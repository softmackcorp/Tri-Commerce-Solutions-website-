Privacy Policy

This page explains how information submitted through this website may be handled.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This is a starter template. Before publishing, update it to reflect your actual data collection, hosting, analytics, email, and privacy practices, and review applicable law.

1. Information you provide

If you contact us or request a quote, you may provide your name, email address, company name, requested service, budget, timeline, and project details. Please do not submit passwords, full payment card details, or sensitive personal information through the form.

2. How information may be used

Information you provide may be used to respond to inquiries, prepare proposals, communicate about projects, provide contracted services, maintain business records, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations.

3. Contact form operation

The included contact form is a front-end template that prepares an email using your device's email application. It does not itself store submissions on this website. The site owner must configure a valid contact email before launch. If a separate form provider or CRM is added later, this policy should be updated to describe it.

4. Cookies, analytics, and hosting

This starter website does not intentionally include analytics or advertising tracking scripts. The hosting provider, domain provider, or other technical services may process technical data such as server logs or IP addresses according to their own terms. If you add analytics, advertising, embedded content, or cookies, update this policy accordingly.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell information submitted through the contact form. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the business, deliver services, or comply with law. We retain business communications for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above and applicable recordkeeping requirements.

6. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect business communications and project information. No website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you have provided, subject to legal and contractual obligations. Rights and response requirements may vary by jurisdiction.

8. Children's privacy

This website is intended for business and general audiences and is not designed to collect information from children.

9. Policy updates and contact

This policy may be updated when our practices change. For privacy questions or requests, use our contact page.
