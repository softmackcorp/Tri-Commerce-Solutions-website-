IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. In script.js replace CONTACT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER with the real business email address.
2. The contact form opens the visitor's email application; it does not store or transmit form data to a server. Test it on desktop and mobile. If you want submissions to arrive without the visitor's email app, connect a form backend/provider and update the privacy policy.
3. Review and customize the Terms, Refund Policy, and Privacy Policy for your actual practices and applicable laws. These are general templates, not legal advice.
4. Add your confirmed business contact details, any real business address/phone, and domain once finalized. Do not publish placeholders.
5. The website uses Google Fonts via an external stylesheet; if you prefer no external requests, remove the @import from styles.css and use system fonts.
6. No customer reviews, certifications, client logos, or unverified claims have been fabricated.

DEPLOYMENT
This is a static site. Upload the contents of this folder to a static web host such as Cloudflare Pages. The site has no build step: use the root folder as the output directory. Connect a domain you own after deployment and follow the host's DNS instructions.
