Refund Policy

Our goal is to set clear expectations about project scope, payment, cancellation, and refund requests.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Important: This starter policy must be checked against your actual contracts, payment processor rules, and applicable consumer laws before publishing. Do not promise a refund outcome that differs from your real practice.

1. Digital and custom services

Our services may include custom graphic design, website development, digital marketing, UX/UI design, mobile application development, and web hosting solutions. Because projects can involve reserved time, planning, custom work, and third-party costs, refund eligibility depends on the work performed and the terms agreed before purchase, subject to applicable law.

2. Before work begins

If a client requests cancellation before work has started, any refund of amounts paid will be assessed against the written proposal, invoice, cancellation terms, and any non-recoverable costs already incurred. Any approved refund will be communicated in writing.

3. After work has started

If a project is cancelled after work has begun, charges may apply for work completed, time reserved, approved expenses, and non-refundable third-party fees, as permitted by the project agreement and applicable law. Any refundable balance will be calculated based on the agreed terms and the status of the project.

4. Delivered work and revisions

Clients should raise concerns about a deliverable in writing and identify the issue in relation to the agreed scope. Where revisions are included in the project agreement, we will follow that review process. A change of preference or a request outside the agreed scope does not automatically entitle a client to a refund, subject to applicable law.

5. Hosting and third-party charges

Domain registration, hosting, software subscriptions, advertising spend, stock assets, app-store fees, and other third-party charges may be governed by the provider's own policies and may be non-refundable. We will identify such costs in the proposal or invoice where applicable.

6. How to request a refund

Send a written request through our contact page with your name, invoice or project reference, date of payment, service purchased, and a clear explanation of the request. Do not send full card numbers, passwords, or other sensitive payment credentials.

7. Review and processing

We will review the request against the project agreement, work completed, relevant payment records, and applicable requirements. If a refund is approved, it will generally be sent to the original payment method where supported. The time for funds to appear may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

8. Legal rights and contact

This policy does not limit rights that cannot legally be excluded. If a separate signed agreement or mandatory law gives you additional rights, those rights apply. For questions, please contact us through the website.
